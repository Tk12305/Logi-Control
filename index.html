With your Logi Bolt receiver connected, use Open Solaar to pair devices and change the settings that Solaar detects. This app keeps the safe system status in one place.

    

    

      
Advanced controls

      
Use Solaar for receiver pairing, supported button actions, gesture rules and per-device settings. In Wayland, button-diversion rules require extra permissions; the app keeps them separate so ordinary scrolling and buttons continue to work.
